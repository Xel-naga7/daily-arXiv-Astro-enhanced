TL;DR: 本文研究了闪烁效应对快速射电暴（FRB）21厘米氢吸收线探测能力的影响。通过构建薄散射屏的衍射闪烁模型，发现当闪烁去相关带宽与吸收线宽度尺度显著不同时，吸收轮廓在高信噪比下可被探测；重复暴需间隔大于衍射闪烁时标才能通过叠加改善信号；目前需叠加约1000次暴发才能达到5σ显著性。

Motivation: 此前已有研究关注探测器噪声对提取中性氢21厘米吸收线的困难，但闪烁效应对该吸收线可探测性的影响尚不明确。本文旨在系统测试闪烁在HI吸收线探测中的作用，并刻画实际FRB可能探测到21厘米线的参数区间。

Method: 构建了基于薄散射屏的衍射闪烁高效模拟模型；将重复FRB位置与银河系CO分子云观测星表交叉比对；通过理论建模与数值模拟分析闪烁去相关带宽、吸收线宽度、信噪比及暴发叠加策略对探测能力的影响。

Result: 闪烁主导的高信噪比下，当闪烁去相关带宽与吸收线宽度显著不同时吸收轮廓可探测；重复暴叠加需间隔大于衍射闪烁时标；FRB 20180916B视线方向可能与银河系分子云相交；当前及计划中灵敏度的望远镜需叠加约1000次暴发才能达到5σ显著性的探测。

Conclusion: 闪烁是影响FRB 21厘米吸收线探测的关键因素之一，合理的观测策略（如选择合适的闪烁去相关带宽匹配、优化重复暴叠加间隔）有助于提升探测能力；提高探测器灵敏度将助力探测FRB宿主星系、中介星系或高红移迷你暗晕中的HI云。

Abstract: The 21-cm absorption line of neutral hydrogen has been a long hypothesized observational feature of the spectra of Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs). The difficulties associated with detector noise in extracting HI absorption have been previously studied. We test the role that scintillation plays in the HI absorption line's detectability, and characterize the regimes where a realistic FRB may yield the 21-cm line. We build an efficient model to simulate diffractive scintillation arising from FRB passage through a thin scattering screen. We find that the absorption profile is detectable in a scintillation-dominated high signal-to-noise spectrum if the scintillation decorrelation bandwidth differs significantly in scale from the width of the absorption profile. Active repeaters also enable favorable conditions as the absorption signal improves when repeat bursts are stacked. Repeat bursts must be separated in time by more than the diffractive scintillation timescale, otherwise flux modulations with frequency are correlated. By cross-referencing repeating FRB positions with an observational catalog of Milky Way molecular clouds detected in CO, we find that the sightline to FRB 20180916B may intersect a Galactic molecular cloud. For currently operating and planned sensitive telescopes, the presence of both scintillation and noise requires $\gtrsim 1000$ bursts to be stacked to detect the HI absorption line at a $5σ$ significance. Improvement in detector sensitivities will help probe HI clouds intersected by FRBs in the host or intervening galaxies, or in high-redshift minihalos.

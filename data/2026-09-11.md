### [1] [Strong Black Hole Natal Kicks in Magnetized Accretion-Powered Explosions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10694)
*Sean E. Li,Ore Gottlieb,Brian D. Metzger*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 强磁场快速旋转塌缩星形成的黑洞可获得10²–10³ km/s的 natal kick，其幅度与方向由非对称电磁外流、磁通爆发和喷流驱动非球形抛射物的引力共同决定。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 黑洞 natal kick 通常被认为弱于中子星 kick（因吸积质量更大），且孤立黑洞电磁辐射暗弱、观测约束困难。本文旨在通过三维模拟检验磁化塌缩星能否产生显著黑洞反冲。

Method: 采用最长约30秒的三维广义相对论磁流体力学（3D GRMHD）模拟，研究快速旋转大质量恒星塌缩（collapsar）形成的黑洞，在强大尺度磁场作用下如何获得 natal kick。

Result: 黑洞可获得10²–10³ km/s的 natal kick。非对称电磁外流、磁通爆发及非球形抛射物引力共同决定 kick 幅度；自旋更快的黑洞获得更强且更自旋对齐的 kick，主要来自非对称喷流驱动抛射物的引力牵引；延迟进入磁阻状态会产生更不对称外流和更强反冲。

Conclusion: 磁化塌缩星中大规模的磁场是伽马射线暴喷流及高能超新星的关键要素，因此 natal kick 可能是磁化塌缩星的自然结果，并显著影响致密星团中黑洞留存、双星存活、自旋-轨道失对齐，以及磁化塌缩星在质量间隙内产生高自旋黑洞的可行性。

Abstract: Asymmetric stellar explosions impart an impulse, or natal kick, to their compact remnants by linear momentum conservation. Black hole (BH) natal kicks are often assumed to be weaker than neutron star kicks because of greater mass accretion, and they are harder to constrain observationally because isolated BHs are electromagnetically faint. Using 3D general-relativistic magnetohydrodynamic simulations lasting up to ~30 s, we show that BHs formed from the collapse of rapidly rotating massive stars (collapsars) threaded by strong large-scale magnetic fields can acquire large natal kicks 10^2--10^3 km/s. Asymmetric electromagnetic outflows, magnetic-flux eruptions, and the gravitational pull of aspherical ejecta shape the kick magnitude, and their relative contributions depend on the progenitor structure, magnetic-flux history, and BH spin. More rapidly spinning BHs receive stronger, more nearly spin-aligned kicks, primarily through the gravitational pull of asymmetric jet-driven ejecta. Delayed transitions to the magnetically arrested state produce more asymmetric outflows and can generate even stronger recoils. Because the large-scale magnetic flux required in our models is also a key ingredient of relativistic gamma-ray burst jets and their associated energetic, jet-driven supernovae, natal kicks may be a natural consequence of magnetized collapsars. Such kicks could substantially alter BH retention in dense stellar environments, binary survival, spin-orbit misalignment, and the viability of magnetized collapsars in producing highly spinning BHs within the pair-instability mass gap.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 1) 采用Ashtekar变量的均匀SU(2)规范理论描述经典宇宙学扇区；2) 施加"散度约束"（将规范固定为均匀规范，经典层面恢复有限维相空间）与"对角约束"（强制度规对角性）；3) 构造一般宇宙学模型对应的量子算符，并以全息（holonomies）与通量（fluxes）给出合适的正则化；4) 借助约化量子圈引力（RQCG）技术，检验Bianchi I与Bianchi IX模型的均匀自旋网络态。

Result: 证明了一类特殊的均匀自旋网络态（描述Bianchi I和Bianchi IX模型）满足量子规范固定约束。由于所选态具有有效阿贝尔结构，该结果在量子层面建立了具有完整SU(2)规范对称性的宇宙学理论与标准LQC之间的联系。

Conclusion: 该工作成功在量子层面实现了规范固定条件，表明完整SU(2)规范对称的宇宙学理论与标准圈量子宇宙学存在量子层面的对应关系，为连接两种表述提供了新途径。

Abstract: In this work, we implement at the quantum level the gauge-fixing conditions that relate the homogeneous SU(2) gauge theory of Ashtekar variables, describing the classical cosmological sector, to the usual minisuperspace formulation. We impose the so-called divergence constraint, which fixes the gauge to the homogeneous one and, at the classical level, recovers a finite-dimensional phase space, together with the diagonal constraint, which imposes the diagonality of the metric and has already been extensively studied in the literature. We construct the corresponding quantum operators for general cosmological models and provide a suitable regularization in terms of holonomies and fluxes. We then show that a special class of homogeneous spin-network states describing the Bianchi I and Bianchi IX models satisfies the quantum gauge-fixing constraints, using techniques developed in Reduced Quantum Loop Gravity. This provides a quantum-level link between the cosmological theory with full SU(2) gauge symmetry and standard Loop Quantum Cosmology, by reason of the effective Abelian structure of the selected states.

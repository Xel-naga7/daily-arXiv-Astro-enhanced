TL;DR: 基于NuSTAR与XMM-Newton对JWST北黄极时域场近5年的准同时X射线观测，本文报告了112个NuSTAR源和453个XMM-Newton源的光变结果。

Motivation: 利用长期、准同时的NuSTAR（硬X射线）与XMM-Newton（软X射线）联合监测，系统刻画该深场中X射线源的变异性及其与光度、计数统计的关系。

Method: 使用2019年起约5年连续的NuSTAR（累计3.5 Ms）与XMM-Newton（228 ks）准同时观测的X射线测光数据，对探测到的112个NuSTAR源与453个XMM-Newton源进行多能段光变分析，采用≥99%置信度判据识别变源。

Result: 4个NuSTAR源和11个XMM-Newton源在至少一个能段以≥99%置信度表现出光变；有红移测量的变源中，74%的变源亮度高于巡天灵敏度极限的5倍；计数>400的源中约1/3有光变，而计数较少的源中仅4个显示变异性。

Conclusion: 变异性与光度正相关，探测到的光变更倾向于出现在高信噪比（高计数）源中；光变时标难以确定，但光变振幅在更长时标上趋于更大。

Abstract: NuSTAR and XMM-Newton have observed the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) North Ecliptic Pole (NEP) Time-Domain Field (TDF) for almost five contiguous years starting in 2019. In that time, the NEP X-ray survey has accumulated 3.5 Ms and 228 ks of quasi-simultaneous NuSTAR and XMM-Newton observations, respectively. This paper presents variability results for the 112 NuSTAR and 453 XMM-Newton sources detected in this field, based solely on the X-ray photometric data. Four NuSTAR sources and 11 XMM-Newton sources varied in at least one band at >=99% confidence. The sources with redshift measurements show a relationship between luminosity and variability with 74% of variable sources brighter than 5x the sensitivity limit of the survey. This is supported by about 1/3 of sources with more than 400 counts detected being variable and only 4 sources with fewer counts showing variability. Variability timescales are not well determined, but variability amplitude tends to be larger on longer timescales.

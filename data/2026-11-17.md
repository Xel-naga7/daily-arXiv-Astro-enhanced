Motivation: BBH绕SMBH运行时会在每次经过SMBH后方发生重复引力透镜，而此前关于轨道诱导的引力波调制研究促使作者深入探究演化层级三体系统中的重复透镜效应；同时需要明确外轨道动力学演化（而非固定的圆轨道无进动近似）对透镜事件识别的必要性。

Method: 外轨道采用后牛顿动力学描述，inspiral波形包含运动源效应与de Sitter进动；采用事件局域的质点透镜模型追踪源-透镜对准关系与引力波频率的变化；针对为期一年的LISA观测，分析透镜事件的识别、Fisher矩阵参数不确定度估计，以及忽略透镜时的波形残差。

Result: 外轨道演化会改变连续透镜事件的时间与放大率，并能将初始不利的构型带入透镜区域；固定圆轨道、无进动近似在相当比例的选定透镜系统中会遗漏所有透镜事件；不确定度的中位分数缩减在外轨道倾角与SMBH自旋上最为显著；对部分高于探测阈值的系统，即使对未透镜模型参数作局域调整，透镜诱导的波形差异仍持续存在。

Conclusion: 重复引力透镜可在LISA对层级BBH的观测中，为SMBH性质（如自旋）与外轨道几何提供互补性约束，强调了在波形建模中计入外轨道演化与进动的必要性。

Abstract: Gravitational waves (GWs) from a stellar-mass binary black hole (BBH) orbiting a supermassive black hole (SMBH) can experience repeated gravitational lensing as the BBH passes behind the SMBH. Our previous studies of orbit-induced GW modulation motivate this investigation of repeated lensing in evolving hierarchical triple systems. We investigate how eccentricity, inclination, and relativistic precession of the outer orbit affect successive lensing events and parameter inference. The outer orbit follows post-Newtonian dynamics, and the inspiral waveform includes moving-source effects and de Sitter precession. An event-local point-mass lensing model tracks the changing source--lens alignment and GW frequency. For a one-year LISA observation, we examine lensing-event identification, Fisher-matrix estimates of parameter uncertainties, and waveform residuals when lensing is omitted. Our results show that outer-orbit evolution changes the timing and amplification of successive lensing events and can bring initially unfavorable configurations into the lensing region. A fixed circular, nonprecessing approximation misses all lensing events for a substantial fraction of the selected lensed systems. The median fractional reductions in uncertainty are largest for the outer-orbit inclination and SMBH spin. For some systems above the adopted detection threshold, lensing-induced waveform differences persist after local adjustments to the parameters of the unlensed model. Repeated lensing can thus provide complementary constraints on SMBH properties and outer-orbit geometry in LISA observations of hierarchical BBHs.

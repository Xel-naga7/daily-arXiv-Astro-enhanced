TL;DR: 该文以正则AdS黑洞作为正则化几何，通过对偶CFT两点函数的奇点结构研究黑洞奇点消解的全息印记：原本对应"反弹零测地线"的奇点被一个与复（而非实）零测地线相关的平滑凸起取代，其位置由OPE结构分析所支持。

Motivation: 黑洞奇点被视界遮蔽，难以判断其是否被量子引力或弦效应消解；对AdS-Schwarzschild黑洞，已知对偶CFT两点函数存在与"经奇点反弹的零测地线"相关的奇点，因此可从边界关联函数侧面探测内部奇点是否被正则化，以及正则内部信息如何编码在边界理论中。

Method: 选取正则（无奇点）AdS黑洞作为奇点消解的正则化几何；用WKB分析计算其准正规模，并由这些模式读出对偶CFT的两点函数；进一步分析两点函数算符乘积展开（OPE）的结构以定位奇异结构。

Result: 对奇异黑洞而言源于反弹零测地线的奇点，在正则黑洞情形下被一个平滑的"凸起"所替代，且该凸起关联于复零测地线而非实零测地线；该复测地线奇点的位置与两点函数OPE结构的分析结果一致。

Conclusion: 结果为黑洞奇点消解提供了全息特征信号，并阐明了正则内部的信息如何编码在边界关联函数中，说明准正规模—两点函数—OPE这一链条可用于刻画内部几何的正则化效应。

Abstract: The black hole singularity is hidden behind its horizon, and it is difficult to determine whether it is resolved by quantum gravity or stringy effects. For an AdS-Schwarzschild black hole, it is known that the two-point function of the dual conformal field theory has a singularity associated with a null geodesic that bounces off the black hole singularity. In this paper, we investigate how the resolution of the black hole singularity is encoded in the dual conformal field theory by considering regular AdS black holes as regularized geometries. Using WKB analysis, we compute the quasinormal modes of regular AdS black holes and read off the dual two-point functions from the modes. We find that the singularity corresponding to the bouncing null geodesic for singular black holes is replaced by a smooth bump associated with a complex, rather than real, null geodesic. The location of the complex geodesic singularity is supported by analyzing the structure of the operator product expansion for the two-point function. Our results provide a holographic signature of black hole singularity resolution and clarify how information about the regular interior is encoded in boundary correlation functions.

### [1] [Hidden structures in the UHE sky: unveiling potential sources via spectral signatures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12174)
*Mateus Zeferino Rennó,Cainã de Oliveira*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文提出一种基于能谱泛函 μ_E 的方向搜寻指标，用于探测 UHECR 的局域谱特征，并给出能量加权显著性公式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统 UHECR 方向搜寻依赖粒子通量的方向性超出，虽已成熟，但无法探测局域谱特征；而这类谱特征一旦测得可为源的性质提供约束。

Method: 定义给定到达方向能谱的泛函 μ_E，证明其可将天空区域的通量归一化与谱形状解耦；推导 Li-Ma 显著性公式在能量加权指标下的推广；在探索性蒙特卡洛模型中采用最大似然分析。

Result: μ_E 在数学上实现通量归一化与谱形状解耦，成为方向依赖谱的独立探针；推广的显著性公式可用于评估其统计显著性；蒙特卡洛分析表明 μ_E 能识别标准通量图中不明显的硬谱区域。

Conclusion: μ_E 可提升发现潜力，作为 UHECR 潜在源的互补探针，与常规通量图搜寻形成补充。

Abstract: The search for the origin of ultrahigh-energy cosmic rays (UHECRs) typically relies on identifying directional excesses in the particle flux. While this approach is well established, it cannot detect localized spectral features, which could provide constraints on the sources of UHECRs if measured. In this work, we propose a complementary metric for directional searches, based on a functional $μ_E$ of the energy spectrum in a given arrival direction. We demonstrate that $μ_E$ mathematically decouples the flux normalization of a sky region from the spectral shape, providing a distinct probe for direction-dependent spectra. To assess the statistical significance of this metric, we derive a generalization of the Li-Ma significance formula for energy-weighted metrics. Using a maximum-likelihood analysis within an exploratory Monte-Carlo model, we show that $μ_E$ can enhance discovery potential by identifying hard-spectrum regions that are not apparent in standard particle-flux maps, thereby providing a complementary probe of potential UHECR sources.

### [2] [Sultana--Dyer Black Hole Spacetime and $h$-Almost Yamabe Solitons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12019)
*Absos Ali Shaikh,Kamiruzzaman*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: 本文研究Sultana-Dyer黑洞时空上的Yamabe型孤子结构，证明非Killing向量场∂_t可诱导h-几乎Yamabe孤子，并分析了能动张量与多种曲率结构的相容性及能量条件。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在广义Yamabe孤子框架下刻画含时Sultana-Dyer黑洞时空的几何结构，并探讨其物质分布与因果结构的几何对应关系。

Method: 利用SD时空的显式度规与曲率量，构造h-几乎Yamabe孤子并确定h与λ；检验∂_r、∂_θ是否满足Yamabe流条件；分析由类时尘埃与零辐射流体组成的能动张量与Riemann、共形(Weyl)、共圆、共调和射影曲率结构的相容性；并在晚期宇宙膨胀约束下检验零、弱、主导能量条件。

Result: 非Killing向量场∂_t诱导出h-几乎Yamabe孤子；给出其退化为几乎Yamabe孤子的条件；发现∂_r与∂_θ不满足相应Yamabe流条件；能动张量与多种曲率结构相容，显示物质分布与因果结构间的强几何对应；在晚期膨胀约束下，零、弱与主导能量条件被违反。

Conclusion: 研究给出了含时Sultana-Dyer时空在广义Yamabe孤子框架下的几何刻画，揭示了其物质-曲率对应关系及能量条件的破坏特征。

Abstract: This paper investigates Yamabe-type soliton structures on the Sultana-Dyer (SD) black-hole spacetime. By employing the explicit metric and curvature quantities associated with the SD spacetime, we demonstrate that the non-Killing vector field $\partial_t$ induces an $h$-almost Yamabe soliton for explicitly determined functions $h$ and $λ$. We also establish the condition under which this structure reduces to an almost Yamabe soliton and examine why the coordinate vector fields $\partial_r$ and $\partial_θ$ do not satisfy the corresponding Yamabe flow conditions. Furthermore, we show that the energy-momentum tensor, consisting of a timelike dust component and a null radiation fluid, is compatible with the Riemann, conformal (Weyl), concircular, conharmonic, and projective curvature structures. This compatibility reveals a strong geometric correspondence between the matter distribution and the causal structure of the spacetime. However, when the time-dependent scalar-curvature profile is subject to the constraints associated with late-stage cosmic expansion, the null, weak, and dominant energy conditions are violated. These findings provide a geometric characterization of the time-dependent Sultana-Dyer spacetime within the framework of generalized Yamabe solitons.

### [3] [Constraining Ultralight Scalars with Black Hole Binary Mergers in Galactic Nuclei](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12052)
*Majed Khalaf,Eric Kuflik,Alessandro Lenoci,Nicholas Chamberlain Stone,Ofri Telem*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: 超轻标量场在旋转黑洞周围形成超辐射云，可作为双星近距离相遇时的额外能量耗散通道，从而提升黑洞双星形成率与并合率；利用GWTC-5中质量比低于3/10的并合事件率，可探测质量区间[4×10⁻¹⁴, 10⁻¹³] eV的超轻标量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基于黑洞自旋测量的超轻标量探测策略存在局限，需要补充性的、独立于自旋观测的探测途径。超辐射云在密集环境（如星系核）中增强黑洞双星并合率这一效应，为利用引力波并合率统计探测超轻标量提供了新的可能。

Method: 分析超辐射云作为额外能量耗散通道对黑洞双星近距离相遇动力学的影响，进而计算其对并合率的增强效应；统计LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA GWTC-5目录中质量比低于3/10的并合事件率，与理论预言对比以约束超轻标量质量区间。

Result: GWTC-5中质量比小于3/10的并合率可探测质量范围[4×10⁻¹⁴, 10⁻¹³] eV的超轻标量，该区间与基于黑洞自旋测量的现有策略形成互补。

Conclusion: 利用引力波并合率（尤其是小质量比事件率）作为探针，可对超轻标量场参数空间施加约束，为超轻粒子的引力波探测提供了自旋测量之外的新途径。

Abstract: Ultralight scalars can form long-lived, macroscopic bound states around spinning black holes, known as superradiance clouds. These clouds provide an additional channel for energy dissipation during close encounters, enhancing the black hole binary formation and merger rates in dense environments such as galactic nuclei. We show that the rate of mergers with mass ratio below $3/10$ in the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA GWTC-5 catalog can probe ultralight scalars in the mass range $[4\times 10^{-14},10^{-13}]$ eV, complementing existing strategies based on black hole spin measurements.

</details>
